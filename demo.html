Walkthrough: from Rhino file to submission diagram

This 2‑minute narrated demo shows exactly how Shadow Study works inside Rhino — opening your model, selecting geometry, generating the shadow diagrams, and exporting the results.

Key steps

  1. Open your Rhino project and launch the Shadow Study plugin.
  2. Set your sun angles or choose preset equinox/solstice configurations.
  3. Click Generate — diagrams are created instantly within the viewport.
  4. Results are saved automatically in an organized export folder.
Download for Rhino 8

Need help installing?

After download, double‑click the .rhi file to install. The plugin will appear under Tools → Plugins → Shadow Study in Rhino 8. If you have trouble, reach out at hello@shadowstudy.app.
